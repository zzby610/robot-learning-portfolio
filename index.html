Lund Robotics Lab - OpenVLA-style Sim-to-Real Adaptation

Sim-to-real adaptation of OpenVLA-style action-token policies

Lund Robotics Lab • Project Lead • Feb 2025 – May 2025

OpenVLA Magma Visual Target and Motion Annotations Action Tokenization LoRA UR5e
  • Conducted a sim-to-real study adapting OpenVLA-style action-token policies from LIBERO simulation data to a UR5e real-robot manipulation setup.
  • Implemented a 256-bin action-tokenization pipeline mapping continuous 7D end-effector commands into discrete tokens, followed by LoRA fine-tuning with LIBERO data and 183 real-robot trajectories.
  • Used Set-of-Mark-style visual target annotations and trajectory-based motion cues to organize action-grounded supervision for downstream adaptation.
  • Deployed the policy through ROS 2 with asynchronous inference, achieving 61% task success and analyzing discretization errors, inference latency, visual shift, and rollout instability.
Evaluation details: UR5e real-robot pick-and-place deployment with 183 real-robot fine-tuning trajectories; no separate rollout-count metadata was found in the available project files.
Research value: a compact sim-to-real study covering action representation, limited real-robot fine-tuning, deployment constraints, and failure analysis.
