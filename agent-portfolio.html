PythonReAct-style orchestrationMCPStreamlitConfluence & Jira

The problem

Useful context lives across pages, issues, and planning information. An assistant needs to retrieve the right evidence, choose the right workflow, and keep people in control of changes.

My role

Agent architecture, context and retrieval logic, enterprise integrations, user interface, deployment, and reliability evaluation.

01

Route & execute

Built a custom router-executor architecture with modular skills and on-demand instructions.

02

Ground the context

Integrated Node.js MCP servers, page parsing, terminology grounding, and structured session memory.

03

Keep people in control

Added approval for write operations and fallbacks when the available context was incomplete.

04

Deliver & evaluate

Built the Streamlit interface, deployed on AWS with Docker and Kubernetes, and evaluated with internal users.
